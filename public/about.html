Waikīkī Lineup

A free surf game you play in your browser. Paddle out at Canoes or Queens, read the sets, catch a wave, and share the lineup with other surfers.

Paddle out

The hour is real

The sun in the game follows real Hawaiʻi time. If it is dark in Honolulu, it is dark in the water, and you surf by moonlight.

How to play

  • Hold W to paddle. Steer with A and D.
  • When a wave is a few seconds away, point the nose at the beach and paddle.
  • Press Space when the wave lifts you, and you pop up.
  • Press X to duck dive or turtle roll under whitewater.
  • On a phone, steer with your left thumb and hold your right thumb down to paddle. Swipe up to pop up and down to dive.
  • Esc or the Menu button opens the settings, where you can change the keys.

Sharing waves

The surfer closest to the peak has the wave. Taking off in front of them is a drop-in, and it costs you Respect.

A note on place

Waikīkī is a real place, and people have surfed there for many generations. This game tries to treat it with care. Place names keep their ʻokina and kahakō, the buildings are generic, and no real person or business appears in it. If something here is wrong, please say so.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your name and board are saved only in your own browser.

Open source

The code is free to use, copy, and improve under the MIT license. Everything you see and hear is generated in code, except the shapes of Lēʻahi and the Koʻolau, which come from public USGS elevation data.
